Trick Shot Pool – Support

Trick Shot Pool is a neon trick-shot puzzle game for iPhone: 69 hand-made levels, a Daily challenge, Endless, and two-player modes.

Need help or have an idea?

Use the Feedback button in the game, or email hartwellsjoinery@gmail.com. We're actively working on the game and read every message.

Common questions

Restoring purchases: tap your diamonds at the top of the menu to open the Diamonds shop, then tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple account you bought with.

Lost progress: progress is saved on your iPhone, so deleting the app deletes your progress.

Ads: ads are optional rewarded videos you choose to watch.
