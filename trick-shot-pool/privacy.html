Trick Shot Pool – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Trick Shot Pool ("the app") is made by Dylan Hartwell, a developer based in Wales, UK. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. If you have any questions, email hartwellsjoinery@gmail.com.

What we don't collect

The app has no sign-up or account. We don't ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and we don't run our own servers that collect information about you.

Game progress

Your levels, stars, coins, diamonds, cues and settings are stored only on your device. If you delete the app, this progress is deleted too.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows optional rewarded video ads (for example, to see a "Show me" shot or double a reward). These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type, operating system, and how you interact with ads.

  • Consent: if you are in the UK, EU or EEA, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can choose non-personalised ads.
  • Tracking: on iPhone, Apple asks whether you allow the app to track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", Google does not receive your advertising identifier and ads are not personalised using it. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Google's use of this information is covered by its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

Purchases (diamonds and the Season Pass) are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check which purchases you have made; it receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from Apple, but not your name or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the app, it opens your own email app. We receive only what you choose to send, including your email address, and use it only to read and reply to your feedback.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we don't hold personal information about you, there is usually nothing for us to access or delete. For information held by Google or RevenueCat, use the links above. If you are in the UK or EU you can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
